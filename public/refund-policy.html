Last updated: August 31, 2026

30-Day Off-Grid Guarantee

We want you to actually use SunBreeze, not just own it. If the Base Camp Bundle doesn't earn a permanent spot in your kit, contact us within 30 days of your purchase date for a full refund of the purchase price.

How to Request a Refund

Orders placed through this site are processed by ClickBank, our retailer of record. You can request a refund one of two ways:

  • Through ClickBank directly, using the order confirmation email you received at checkout, or
  • By emailing us at koubre@gmail.com with your order number, and we'll process it with ClickBank on your behalf.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and are typically processed by ClickBank within a few business days of approval.

Digital Guides

The Off-Grid Battery Handbook, Keep It Spinning, and the Quick-Reference Card are included as bonuses with your purchase of the physical bundle and are not sold or refunded separately.

Damaged or Defective Items

If your fan or power bank arrives damaged or stops working outside of normal wear, contact us right away with your order number and a description of the issue. We'll make it right — a replacement or refund — and this is separate from, and in addition to, the 30-day guarantee above.

Questions

Reach us any time at koubre@gmail.com. We reply to support requests within one business day.
