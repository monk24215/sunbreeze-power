Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SunBreeze ("we," "us," "our"), a product line of WiredHowse Builders Cooperative, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or purchase SunBreeze products.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, checkout and payment are processed by ClickBank, our retailer of record. ClickBank collects the information necessary to complete your purchase, including your name, email address, shipping address, and payment details. We do not receive or store your full payment card information at any point.

We receive limited order information from ClickBank — such as your name, email address, and shipping address — in order to fulfill your order, provide customer support, and deliver the digital guides included with your purchase.

If you contact us directly by email, we retain that correspondence in order to respond to you and provide support.

2. How We Use Information

  • To process and ship your order
  • To deliver digital products (ebooks and bonus materials) to you
  • To respond to support requests and honor the 30-day guarantee
  • To send order-related communications (confirmations, shipping updates, support replies)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Payment processing and order management for this site are handled by ClickBank. Your use of ClickBank's checkout is also governed by ClickBank's own Privacy Policy. This site may also use basic website analytics to understand traffic; any such analytics data is aggregated and not used to personally identify you.

4. Data Retention

We retain order and support correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill orders, honor guarantees, and comply with applicable recordkeeping obligations.

5. Your Choices

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you, contact us at the email below. Note that ClickBank, as the payment processor, maintains its own records of your transaction independently of us.

6. Contact
